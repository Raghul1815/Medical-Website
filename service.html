Cardiac Arrest
Step by Step First Aid Instructions:

1. Call Emergency Services Immediately: Dial the emergency number and provide clear information on the location and situation.

2. Check Responsiveness: Gently shake the person and ask if they’re okay. If unresponsive, proceed to CPR.

3. Begin CPR: Place hands in the center of the chest, press down firmly and quickly (100120 compressions per minute), and allow full chest recoil.

4. Deliver Rescue Breaths if Trained: After every 30 compressions, give 2 rescue breaths if trained in CPR.

5. Use an AED if Available: Turn on the AED and follow the voice prompts until emergency services arrive.

Heart Attack

Step by Step First Aid Instructions:
1. Call Emergency Services Right Away: Provide details on location and symptoms, such as chest pain or difficulty breathing.

2. Encourage the Person to Rest: Have them sit down calmly, preferably in a reclined position.

3. Administer Aspirin (if safe): Give a 325 mg aspirin tablet to chew if the person is not allergic or contraindicated.

4. Loosen Tight Clothing: This can help ease breathing and relieve discomfort.

5. Stay with the Person: Keep them calm and observe for worsening symptoms until emergency help arrives.

Stroke

Step by Step First Aid Instructions:
1. Call Emergency Services Immediately: Time is critical, so call at the first sign of stroke symptoms.

2. Lay the Person Down with Head Elevated: This helps blood flow and can reduce symptoms while awaiting help.

3. Check for FAST Symptoms: Note if the Face droops, Arm weakness occurs, Speech is slurred, and Time of symptom onset.

4. Keep the Person Comfortable and Still: Avoid movement and keep them calm.

5. Monitor Vital Signs: Watch for breathing and responsiveness until professionals arrive.

Seizure

Step by Step First Aid Instructions:
1. Clear the Area: Move any objects away to prevent injury.

2. Place a Soft Object Under the Head: A folded jacket or cushion works well to support the head.

3. Do Not Restrain Movements: Let the seizure run its course, which is usually under two minutes.

4. Stay Calm and Time the Seizure: If it lasts over 5 minutes, call emergency services.

5. Check Responsiveness After the Seizure: Help the person to a safe sidelying position once it’s over and they begin to wake.

Anaphylaxis

Step by Step First Aid Instructions:
1. Administer Epinephrine Immediately: Use an autoinjector (EpiPen) in the outer thigh, holding for 510 seconds.

2. Call Emergency Services Immediately: Time is critical for severe allergies.

3. Have the Person Sit Up and Stay Still: Encourage calmness and breathing, but limit movement.

4. Loosen Tight Clothing: This may ease breathing and prevent discomfort.

5. Monitor for Breathing or Swelling Changes: Prepare to use a second epinephrine dose if symptoms persist after 515 minutes, per medical advice.


Asthma Attack

Step by Step First Aid Instructions:
1. Help Administer Their Inhaler: Shake the inhaler and give one puff; wait 30 seconds, then repeat as necessary.

2. Encourage Slow Breathing: Inhale deeply and exhale slowly to help reduce panic and control breathing.

3. Place the Person in a Comfortable Position: Sitting upright can make breathing easier.

4. Offer Water if Needed: Small sips can help relax the airway.

5. Seek Help if Symptoms Worsen: Call emergency services if the inhaler doesn’t relieve symptoms.

Hypothermia

Step by Step First Aid Instructions:
1. Move the Person to a Warm Area: Take them indoors or to a sheltered location.

2. Remove Wet Clothing: Replace with dry clothes or wrap in blankets to insulate body heat.

3. Provide Warm, NonAlcoholic Fluids: Only give fluids if the person is alert and conscious.

4. Apply Warm Compresses: Use to the neck, chest, and groin but avoid direct heat.

5. Monitor Breathing and Body Temperature: Call emergency services if the person remains cold or is unresponsive.


Heatstroke

Step by Step First Aid Instructions:
1. Call Emergency Services: Report the location and symptoms immediately.

2. Move to a Cooler Area: Shade or an airconditioned place works best.

3. Apply Cold Compresses to Key Areas: Place on the neck, armpits, and groin.

4. Fan the Person: Increasing air circulation helps to cool the body.

5. Give Cool Water if Alert: Small sips help, but avoid excessive amounts.

Choking

StepbyStep First Aid Instructions:
1. Encourage Coughing: If they can speak, allow them to try to clear the object.

2. Ask if They Need Help: If they can’t breathe, prepare to assist.

3. Perform the Heimlich Maneuver: Stand behind, place fists above the navel, and thrust upwards firmly.

4. Continue until Dislodged or Help Arrives: Repeat until the object clears or help comes.

5. Call Emergency Services if Necessary: If the person loses consciousness, call for assistance immediately.

Diabetic Emergency

Step by Step First Aid Instructions:
1. Offer a Quick Source of Sugar: Juice, candy, or glucose tablets work best.

2. Encourage Small Sips of Water: Helps to stabilize after sugar intake.

3. Monitor for Improvement: Watch for signs of improved alertness and stability.

4. Provide Additional Sugar if Needed: If symptoms persist, another serving can help.

5. Call for Medical Help if No Improvement: Emergency help may be needed if symptoms continue.

Bleeding

StepbyStep First Aid Instructions:
1. Apply Direct Pressure to the Wound: Use a clean cloth or gauze to press firmly.

2. Elevate the Injured Area if Possible: Helps to reduce blood flow to the wound.

3. Apply Additional Layers if Needed: Place more cloth or gauze over the wound if bleeding continues; do not remove the initial layer.

4. Secure with a Bandage if Available: Wrap tightly enough to control bleeding.

5. Seek Immediate Medical Help: Call emergency services if bleeding is uncontrollable or severe.

Burns

Step by Step First Aid Instructions:
1. Cool the Burn with Running Water: Use cool (not cold) water for 1015 minutes.

2. Cover with a Sterile, Nonstick Bandage: Avoid applying creams or ointments.

3. Remove Tight Clothing or Jewelry Near the Burn: Prevents swellingrelated issues.

4. Elevate the Burned Area if Possible: Reduces swelling and throbbing.

5. Seek Medical Help for Severe Burns: 2nd or 3rddegree burns, especially on face, hands, or joints, require urgent care.

broken bornes

StepbyStep First Aid Instructions:
1. Immobilize the Area: Keep the injured limb in the position you found it.

2. Apply Ice to Reduce Swelling: Wrap ice in a cloth and apply for 1015 minutes.

3. Use a Splint if Available: Secure the limb with a splint to prevent further injury.

4. Elevate if Possible and Safe: Helps to reduce swelling and pain.

5. Get Professional Medical Help: Transport to a hospital for evaluation and treatment.
